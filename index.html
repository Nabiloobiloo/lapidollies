The Proposition

Lapidollies makes emotion collectible.

Lapidollies is a soft-luxury kawaii collectible IP where semi-precious gemstones come to life as tiny emotional companions. Each Dollie combines a gemstone, a signature color, a feeling and a visual motif — creating a character language that reads instantly on shelf and scales naturally across toys, plush, charms, stickers, stationery and lifestyle accessories.

A premium, giftable universe built for collecting — and designed to be felt.

Brand Idea

A gemstone universe with emotional clarity.

Lapidollies transforms gemstone symbolism into a highly recognizable character system. Every Dollie is built from one clear formula: a stone, a color, a feeling, a motif and a toy-friendly silhouette.

This gives the brand both creative consistency and commercial flexibility — allowing each character to stand alone, while making the full collection feel coherent, desirable and easy to expand.

One gemstone. One feeling. One Dollie to collect, gift and remember.

The Promise

Soft luxury for the collectible generation.

Lapidollies offers a softer alternative to action-led character IPs: a world of tiny companions designed around comfort, beauty, emotional choice and collectibility.

The brand is cute without being disposable, premium without feeling distant, and emotionally warm without losing commercial clarity. Each Dollie is designed to be instantly appealing as an illustration, but also simple enough to become a physical product.

Brand Architecture

One rule keeps the universe coherent.

One gemstone creates one canonical Dollie. Rare, seasonal and premium releases remain variants of that same character — protecting recognition while creating reasons to recollect.

This structure allows Lapidollies to grow through waves, rarity levels, finishes, seasonal editions and premium collections without fragmenting the brand.

Character Design System

Every Dollie speaks the same product language.

Lapidollies characters are built from a shared visual grammar that protects brand recognition while allowing each Dollie to feel distinct. The system is designed to survive translation across physical formats — from illustrated character art to vinyl toys, plush dolls, acrylic charms, stickers, pins and packaging.

01 · Silhouette

Oversized bows, compact proportions and a strong jewel focal point create instant toy recognition.

02 · Color

Each Dollie owns a dominant gemstone palette that carries through every product format.

03 · Motif

Heart, sun, star, clover, drop, moon and prism motifs give each character a clear visual signature.

04 · Emotion

Every character offers a feeling-led reason to choose, gift and collect.

05 · Format

The same identity codes translate into vinyl, plush, charms, stickers, collector cards and retail displays.
